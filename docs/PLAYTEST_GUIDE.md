# Endless Forms: playtest guide

*For the designer and colleagues (D6 decision 4). Written 23 October 2026, at the end of session 4. It covers the 23 rebuilt (v2) chapters in the shipped file. Each chapter takes about 5 minutes. The scripts come from each unit's log in `docs/changes/wave2/<id>.md`, updated for this session's simplification (D6 decisions 9 and 10).*

---

## 1. How to open the game

1. **Get the file.** It is `games/endless-forms/Endless_Forms_v2.html` in the repository: one file of about 3.8 MB. Download it from GitHub (branch `claude/evolution-game-critique-9m7vmx`; open the file, then choose **Download raw file**).
2. **Open it in a browser:** Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. Double-clicking the file works. It needs no internet connection and no install.
3. **On a phone:** send yourself the file (by email, AirDrop or cloud storage), then open it in Safari or Chrome. Test on a phone if you can, because the game is designed for phones first.
4. **From the title screen,** press the main button. The first time, it takes you straight into *The Lichen Wood*. After that you get the menu: twelve acts, plus an appendix. Rebuilt chapters open on a three-part description card (**The game / The biology / How to play**) with a big **Play** button.
5. **Go straight to a chapter** by adding `?unit=<id>` to the file's address, for example `…/Endless_Forms_v2.html?unit=raffle`. Add `&level=2` to start at the third level (levels count from 0). Each chapter below lists its id.
6. **Useful switches:**
   - The **?** button in the dock (bottom bar) reopens the description card.
   - **Settings** (menu footer) has Untimed (no clocks), Calm (less motion), Readable (plainer font) and Left-handed.
   - `?teacher=1` turns on teacher mode, and `?v1=1` opens the old (v1) game for comparison.
   - Progress is saved in the browser. A private (incognito) window starts fresh.
7. **Tell us your device and browser**, for example "iPhone 13, Safari" or "Windows laptop, Chrome".

**What "working" means everywhere:** you know what to do from the card and the ghost hand's first demonstration; something on screen responds to every tap; you see the organisms themselves (moths, flies, frogs, cells), with graphs only beside them; each level ends on a card that says what happened in *your* run; and no level leaves you waiting with nothing to do.

**You do not need to play everything at once.** All 23 chapters take about two hours. Three sittings of seven or eight chapters work well. If you have only 20 minutes, play *The Lichen Wood*, *Equilibrium*, *The Raffle* and *The River*.

### Act 0. The Wood

#### The Lichen Wood (`lichen`): a bird in a lichen-covered wood, R = h²S
*4 levels: Too many moths · The Lichen Wood · Mixed diets · Two barks.*
1. Read the card, press Play, and tap moths to feed your four chicks. **Working:** you find the first moth within about 10 s, and pecks feel responsive.
2. **L0:** at dusk, read "300 eggs, room for 30". Does overproduction land without a question being asked?
3. **L1:** watch the survivors' **S** arrow and the young's **R** arrow. The HUD shows **R = h²S**. **Working:** over a few generations the moths visibly come to match the bark.
4. **L2 (the chapter's one prediction):** tap where the young will land. **Working:** you are surprised that they land only about a third of the way towards the survivors (h² ≈ 0.3).
5. **L3, the boss:** two barks and a search image. Did the rare morph's advantage come across without a prediction?

### Act 3. The gene pool

*5 levels: Two copies each · The tumbler · The census · Close relatives · The bait-layer. This is the one chapter allowed more than four levels (D6 decision 1b).*
