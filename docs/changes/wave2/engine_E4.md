## Deviations from API_v2, and why

1. **`isV2(ch)` (`ch.v2 === true`) in every wrap**, not `ch.v2`, because legacy CH_NEST counts visits in `this.v2` (E2's finding). The self-test checks the Nest with `v2 = 12`.
2. **TAKE and body default from `content` on the final card only.** A level card shows `o.take` and `o.body` only when the chapter passes them, so it does not repeat the unit's 20 + 60 words after every level, or give away the boss. The Interesting result still replaces TAKE on any card.
3. **The star row's aria-label names the stars** ("2 of 3 stars: Discover, Transfer"), so screen-reader users hear which stars, not just how many. It sits on the star row (`role="img"`); the card itself is a labelled dialog.
4. **A criterion line** for the first missing star (from `content.stars`, ≤ 15 words) is not in §6.1. Stars mean understanding (D5 decision 8), so the player needs to see what each one asks.
5. **Why? and What if? appear only when there is something to show.** On a final card with no next registered unit, Notebook is the primary and is not listed twice.
6. **`Freezer` vials carry their level and seed.** `list(id)` returns the current level's vials, a fresh start of a level clears them, and `put` ignores a finished run. Without this, a level that froze nothing would offer an earlier level's vial, and the skeleton's last vial (taken after the final breed) would thaw straight into its end card.
7. **`Sim.tick(ch)` returns the current tick.** §6.1 lists it without a description. Turn-based units (`sim.dt = 1`) call `Sim.run(ch, 1)` once per turn. `Sim` also exposes `log`, `replaying`, `thawed` and `done`.
8. **A finished sim does not step,** and live input is ignored during a replay. §6.1 does not say either; both are needed for exact replays.
9. **`Snap` handles all nine typed-array kinds, expandos and non-finite numbers,** not just `$f32` and `$u32`, because Gen genomes are `Float32Array`s with expandos and `WF` returns `Int32Array`s.
10. **`ResultCode.show(code)`** was added for the "K7M 2QX" display. `make` returns the six characters with no space.
11. **When the result code shows** ("class mode") is my reading: the chapter defines `resultBits` and the URL is an assignment link (`?unit=` for this unit plus `?seed=`). Revisit when D2 §12 assignments get their own URL field.
12. **Card action rows on phones.** Four buttons do not fit in one row at 390 px. The three secondary buttons share a row and Next spans the row below; at 1280 px all fit in one row. E2's level-select card is not affected.

## Findings for other packages

- **E2 (`tests/selftest_e2.js` line 119):** "menu: SVG stars, legacy numerals" expects the first menu heading to read "I". With `00_entry.js` in the build it reads "Act 0", which is the D2 act plan this package is meant to install. Everything else in `selftest_e2.py` passes on my build (150 of 152). Suggested fix: accept `'Act 0'` when `TB.ACT_PLAN` exists, or run that check on a legacy-only build.
- **E2 (`boot.js`):** with the stub DOM of `tests/vmgame.js`, `document.querySelectorAll('#sh-tabs button')` matches every button (the stub reads only the last part of a selector), so boot gives `#btn-begin` a Notes-tab `onclick` there. This is harmless in browsers. My self-test puts boot's Begin handler back before clicking. **E7** may want descendant selectors in `vmgame.js`.
- **Architect (`docs/design/v2_chapter_skeleton.js`):** the skeleton freezes a vial after every breed, including the final one, whose state is already over. `Freezer.put` now ignores that vial, so What if? thaws the last playable generation. The skeleton could also freeze at dusk, before breeding, so a lever applies to the breed it changes.
- **E5:** End cards draw `o.chart` on a 200 px canvas in the paper theme. A chapter may pass a LiveGraph spec instead of a function; it is drawn through `Hooks.chart(cv, spec, 'paper')`.
- **E6:** a Hunt-mode v2 chapter should call `End.show(ch, {level, mask, final, …})`. A legacy-shaped `{stars, insight}` call still opens a v2 card (the insight's text becomes the body) but records no stars, because a star count cannot be turned into Discover, Predict and Transfer bits. `Sim.act(ch, a)` logs Hunt taps even without a `sim`.
- **Chapter authors:** call `Sim.start` in `startLevel`, `Sim.run` in `update`, and route every input through `Sim.act`. Freeze with `Freezer.put(this.id, gen, this.sim.snapshot())`, then pass `whatIf: {gen: Freezer.list(this.id).slice(-1)[0].gen, levers}`. Every key in `whatIf.levers` needs a `content.levers` label (≤ 3 words). `content.stars.*` (≤ 15 words) is shown on the card. A `model` card with `level: 0` covers every later level until one with a higher `level` replaces it.
