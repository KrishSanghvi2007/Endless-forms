## Deviations from API_v2, and why

1. **The monkey seeds `R()` and `Math.random`.** The contract does not ask for this. With virtual time it makes each run a pure function of (build, id, seed, viewport), so a failing run can be replayed exactly. Pass `opts.seedR = false` to `__monkey` to keep the old unseeded start.
2. **The monkey also fails v2 runs on `HUD.warnings` and `Dock.warnings`** (E2's dev-build budget warnings), not only on `Lbl` and `Whisper`. A run that opens Notes on a v2 chapter fails with `notes-opened`. A Why? click on the final card is checked for errors and bad text before the run returns `end`.
3. **The stuck check includes chapter state** (see above). A chapter whose clock is the only thing changing still ends as stuck or timeout. `KNOWN` in monkey.py accepts either for `cross`, `landscape` and `double`. `mendel` and `fancier` are off the known list.
4. **`// cosmetic`** is accepted as well as `// ghost-only`, to exempt view-only maths from the Math.* rule. The Math rule applies to **every** v2 file (E3's engine files are clean). The `fillText` rule applies only to chapter files, because `Lbl`, `Pal.token` and `LiveGraph` draw text by design. `Math.cbrt`/`Math.hypot` are warnings outside a sim (E3 uses cbrt for colour).
5. **The lint uses -ise spelling** (realise, organise), as the legacy text does. Oxford -ize is flagged. A teaching panel under 35 words is a warning; over 60 is an error. MODEL fields over 60 words are warnings, since the card is shown in 60-word chunks. A MODEL card covers its level and the levels after it, so only a level-0 card is required.
6. **Bots decide every tick** (`--every K` to slow them). A missing bot hook is a warning, not a failure.
7. **CVD screenshots are made by post-processing**, not with the in-game `?cvd=` filter. Every variant then shows the identical frame, the whole screen is simulated, including the HUD and the dock, and the harness does not depend on E3's preview.
8. **`det_vectors.json` also holds an `R()`-driven gauss/poisson sequence**, because sims depend on those draws as much as on the pure functions.
9. The brief calls it `tests/vclock.js`; the file is `source/vclock.js`, as API_v2 §2 lists it and every harness loads it. It stays there.

## Findings for other packages

- **Architect (`docs/design/v2_chapter_skeleton.js`):** the lint finds four sentences over 18 words: the panel "Each time you eat…" (24), body (21), why.real (22) and why.maths (22). It also finds that `starTraits: ['dark']` has no non-hue channel in `encodings` (hue lint). The bot sweep finds that a **random bot three-stars the boss level** (seed 18 of 1–20): the transfer star (`pred[asks[2]] < hist[0]`) is a coin flip for a random prediction, and discover and predict are easy on dark sand. Units copying the skeleton will inherit both. Suggested fix: score transfer against the expectation with a tolerance, as predict does.

It finds Why?, What if? and lever chips by their text and by `.chips button, button.chip`, and takes the **last** `.card-btns` button as Next level or Next unit. Please keep those shapes. A v2 replay goes through `App.playLevel(ch, 0, {seed})`.

## Not done

- The axe-core accessibility pass (D5 gate 11) needs axe-core embedded offline. It is not in the repo, so it is not built.
- The "≤ 70 words per phone screen" rule is only measured: the monkey reports `maxWords` over `#hud`, `#dock` and `#whisper` for v2. It is not enforced, because cards and the strip make a firm count unreliable.
- `Events.lat()` latency (≤ 200 ms) is not asserted: an act with no fx makes the next unrelated fx look late. Better as a per-unit oracle.
- Hunt-mode v2 chapters cannot be swept by the bots until Hunt actions replay through `Sim` (API_v2 §9.1, later).
- `smoke.py`, `shots3–16.py`, `t*.py` and `dbg*.py` are legacy and untouched.
