# Wave 2, work package E5: the shared widgets (`v2/01_widgets.js`)

Files: `games/endless-forms/source/v2/01_widgets.js` (owned). New self-tests, as API_v2 §2 asks: `tests/selftest_e5.js` (Node vm, through `tests/vmgame.js`) and `tests/selftest_e5.py` (Chromium). The test fixture chapter `e5fix` lives inside `selftest_e5.js`. It is built only into a private copy of `source/`, never into `source/v2`.

Contract: `docs/design/API_v2.md` §7, with D5 decisions 9, 10, 13–16 and 19, D1 §2.1 (behaviour) and D4 §2.2, §4.2, §5, §8 (look). Every name and signature in §7 is in. That includes the items marked "by B2", "by B3" and "later" (`PedigreeView`, `DemeMap`, `PriceBars`, `TreeView` and the `deme`, `price`, `tree` and `genome` charts), as simple first versions. Where I differ from the contract, the change is listed under "Deviations".

## What I built

| Widget | What it does |
|---|---|
| `LiveGraph` | `draw(cv, spec, theme)`, `map(cv, spec)`, `table(spec)` and `sig(spec)`. `Hooks.chart` is set at load. Ten chart types: `trace`, `dots`, `pq`, `tri` (`hw` or `simplex`), `scatter`, `xhud`, `deme`, `price`, `tree`, `genome`. Realised values are solid 3 px lines with the entity's marker (D4 §2.1: A circle, a square, selection triangle, drift hollow circle, mutation plus, migration arrowhead). Expected values are dashed ink 2 px. "You" is a diamond labelled "you". Ink follows the entity (`ink: 'A' \| 'a' \| 'sel' \| 'drift' \| 'mut' \| 'mig' \| 'ink'`), and Paper uses the darker line column. Tick labels are 12 px mono, inside the plot (20 px in projector). End labels are direct, in a right margin, nudged apart; the marker carries the hue and the words stay in text ink. `frame: 'designed'` gives a dashed plot frame, `'real'` a solid one (its `source` goes into the table caption). `target` is a hatched band. Over three hued series gives a warning. Auto ranges are widened to round values. Nothing calls `R()`. `draw` catches its own errors (`LiveGraph.errors`, reported once through `showErr`), so a bad spec cannot stop a chapter through the frame loop. The canvas `aria-label` is "title: summary", and `table` is the "Show data" twin (≤ 60 rows, escaped). `sig` is an FNV hash of the spec's data (functions skipped, typed arrays read) plus `Predict.ver`, so the strip redraws when the ghost moves. |
| `Predict` | `ask({id, chart, kind, axis, start, tol, soft = 6, onCommit})` returns `{active, v, cancel(), commit()}`. Also `overlay(i)`, `reveal(id, expect, tol)`, `err`, `score`, plus `commit(id?)` and `cancel(id?)`. Kinds: `x`, `y`, `pq`, `tri`, `time`. While an ask is open, `Predict` owns `Hooks.chartPointer` and hands back the previous owner on close. **Gesture:** a tap moves the ghost there, a drag moves it, and a tap on the ghost commits. Keys: P focuses, arrows move (1/50 of the axis; Shift ×5; on the triangle, along screen directions), Enter or Space commits, Escape leaves. `Predict.commit()` lets a chapter add a Dock button. The soft timer counts real seconds, only while the stage is live. It never commits while a pointer is down, any move leaves at least 2 s, and it is off in untimed mode (`Save.d.ui.unt`), whatever the chapter passes. The commit goes through `Sim.act(App.ch, {k: 'predict', id, v})`, which logs it for replays. Then `Events.emit('predict', …)` and `onCommit({id, v})`. The tolerance band hatches in over 400 ms (at once in Calm). The look follows D4: a hollow diamond, a 44 px halo with the countdown arc, a dashed guide line, a focus box when keyboard-focused, and "you". In projector mode during a teacher freeze, the diamond is 3× (D4 freeze-and-ask). `err('tri')` is the distance in a unit-side simplex (corner to corner = 1). |
| `GenePoolLens` | `create({items, jar, letters, blocksTaps, key = 'L', names, onChange})` with `mode`, `set`, `cycle`, `draw(c)`, `q()`, plus `counts()` and `blocks()`. GENES draws two lettered tokens per organism (`Pal.token`), one token with the one-copy bar for −1, and split halves for a crowd (r < 10). JARS ports Equilibrium's jar layout. Tokens fly to the jars staggered over ≤ 1.2 s (in place in Calm), with count pills per jar and a `q =` pill for two alleles. `GenePoolLens.control(lens)` is a 44 px segmented radiogroup for `Dock.set([{id, el}])`. Key L cycles the active lens through `Hooks.key`. |
| `Invasion` | `create({rules, you, N, gens, start, w \| payoff, mu, M, from})` with `step`, `run(n)`, `hist`, `done`, `share`, `draw(c, rect)`, `spec()`, plus `save()`. Also `Invasion.run(o)` (D5's name). Every draw goes through `WF.step`. `hist` is plain arrays, so a sim snapshot can hold it (`save()` / `from`). `draw` shows ≤ 200 tokens in four rows, grouped by rule. Two rules are blue and amber. Three or more are paper discs with a pattern and a letter (D4 rule 4). Your lineage has the paper halo. There is a legend row, and a "1 dot = k" chip when N > 200. `spec()` gives your share solid and the start share dashed (the neutral expectation). |
| `Ledger` | `create({n})` with `push({glyph, good, t})`, `draw(c, x, y, {gen, size, cols})` and `items`. Tiles are 28 px. Age shows as fill, not transparency (D4 rule 11): fresh is solid with a 2 px outline, 1–2 is hatched, ≥ 3 is a dashed outline. Every tile keeps a paper ground and an ink outline. A tick means good, a cross means bad. `glyph` may be a string or a draw function. |
| `Pin` | `press(ind, x, y)`, `release()`, `list`, `isPinned`, `unpin`, `clear`, `draw(c, locate, kids)`. A 450 ms hold pins (≤ 4; a fifth replaces the oldest), and a second hold unpins. The pinned look is copied from `ind.ph` (or `ind.g`), so it is fixed for life. `Pin.update` and the filling hold ring run on `Hooks.frame`. Pins survive levels of the same unit and are cleared on exit or when another unit plays. |
| `Evidence` | `create({budget, instruments, hypotheses, power, posterior, truth, n?, words?, onBuy?, onConclude?})` with `buy`, `left`, `bought`, `conclude(hid, conf)` → `{brier, ghost, hit}`, and `mount(el)`. The DOM is keyboard-ordered (D4 §4.2): a budget row of ≤ 20 discs (spent ones hollow), instrument tiles with a cost chip (hatched when unaffordable), a 220 × 20 power gauge (dashed line at 80%, hatched region below it, diamond needle, `role=meter`), hypothesis cards ≥ 88 × 64, five confidence detents (50–90%+), and Conclude. After the conclusion, calibration pips show your Brier and, dashed, the posterior's. |
| `Chips` | `create({bins, chips, odds})` with `add`, `remove`, `dist`, `draw(c, rect)`, `hit(x, y)`, plus `key(k)` (← → choose, ↑ / + add, ↓ / − remove), `left`, `clear`. `Chips.score` and `Chips.expected` are exactly the contract formulas. The croupier's naive odds are dashed columns, the chips are paper discs with an ink rim, and there is a count above each stack. |
| `PedigreeView` | `create({nodes})` with `layout(rect)`, `draw(c)`, `hit`, `tag`, `tags`, `r`, `path`, plus `kin`, `F`, `show(a, b)`, `tap(x, y)`, `key(k)` and `PedigreeView.frac`. **r is exact**: Wright's coefficient from the kinship recursion (φ(a,a) = (1+F)/2, recursing on the younger). It is dyadic, so exact in floating point, for non-inbred pairs, and 2φ/√((1+Fa)(1+Fb)) otherwise. `path` gives one shortest up-and-down path through a common ancestor. Layout: generations as rows, partners from outside the pedigree beside their mate, each further sire of one mother 6 px higher (multiple paternity). Nodes are 14–28 px: circle female, square male, hexagon unknown. Fill: open, half amber for a carrier, amber for affected. Dead or absent nodes have a dashed outline and no fill (D4 rule 10). There are blaze glyphs, an optional genotype token, ear-flag tags (½ solid, ¼ dashed, ⅛ dotted, 0 no border, untagged "?" hatched), a 4 px path with one tick per step and a ½ⁿ chip, a radial four-tag menu with 44 px targets, and roving keyboard focus. |
| `DemeMap` | `draw(c, rect, spec)` and `hit(x, y)`, also the `deme` chart. Discs are 40–56 px by N (scaled down to fit), with a p\|q bar inside. Migration arrows are 1–6 px by m, offset when two-way. |
| `PriceBars` | `draw(c, rect, {between, within, total})`, also the `price` chart. Between is solid selection ink, within is hatched, total is ink. Negative values are handled. |
| `TreeView` | `create({nodes: [{id, parent, label, t}], rooted})` with `spin(id)`, `reroot(id)` (a virtual root on the branch above id, removed at the next reroot) and `draw(c, rect)`, all chainable. Also `hit`, `tips`, and `steps(states)` (Fitch parsimony). It draws a cladogram with aligned tips, or a phylogram when every branch has `t`. |
| `SceneLab` | `open(id)` (from `?lab=<id>&dev=1` via `boot.js`). A fixed panel: one slider per `ch.P` entry with its tag chip (dashed for "designed", ×N for `xN`, and the real value), a seed box (Go / New), level chips, a replay box (Play replay, Copy replay, Copy link). A slider writes `ch.P[k].v` and restarts the level with the same seed. |

**Rules kept.**
- Every hook is a no-op for a legacy chapter (`isV2`, i.e. `ch.v2 === true`).
- Under `?v1=1` nothing is registered except `Hooks.chart`, which only the v2 strip calls.
- No `Math.exp`/`log`/`pow`/`sin`/`cos`/`random` anywhere, and no direct `R()`; Invasion draws through `WF`.
- Top-level code touches only `Hooks`, `Url` and `EXPORT` (legacy). `Pal`, `Fx`, `Live`, `Sim`, `Strip`, `Replay`, `Events`, `Teacher` and `WF` are read inside functions behind `typeof`.
- A build with only `01_widgets.js` (no 00_ files) loads, and its charts draw with fallback inks. Only Invasion then throws a clear "needs 00_wf.js".
- Exports through `EXPORT`: `LiveGraph Predict GenePoolLens Invasion Ledger Pin Evidence Chips PedigreeView DemeMap PriceBars TreeView SceneLab`.

## Deviations from API_v2, and why

1. **How a prediction commits.** The contract gives "drag or tap moves the ghost; keys …; Enter commits", but no pointer commit. I added: **tap the ghost itself to commit** ("tap to place, tap again to commit"). Releasing a drag never commits, so a slip cannot lock in a value. `Predict.commit()` is there for a Dock button, and the soft timer still commits the current ghost.
2. **The band at commit time.** "On commit … the tolerance band hatches in", but the expectation is unknown to the view until the sim scores it. So the band first hatches in around **your** mark (± tol: your catch window, which reveals nothing). `reveal(id, expect, tol)` then moves it onto the expectation and adds a dashed expectation mark. `reveal` returns `{err, hit}`.
3. **`Events` 'predict' is emitted twice**: at commit `{id, kind, value, tol}`, and at reveal with `expect, err, hit` and `reveal: true`. A chapter that never calls `reveal` still logs its commit.
4. **`axis.rev`** (new, optional): a `pq` prediction measured from the right-hand end of the bar, i.e. a share of the second allele. See "Findings" for why the skeleton needs it.
5. **The phone strip opens for an ask.** On a phone, a collapsed strip chart is about 180 × 78 px, too small to drag on precisely. `ask` expands the strip (`Strip.open(true)`) and closes it 1.5 s after the last commit unless another ask follows. Opt out with `expand: false`.
6. **`Predict.overlay(i)`** returns the whole ask record, a superset of `{v, committed, band}`: `kind, ax, expect, active, soft, left, focus, hatch, err, hit`. A new ask on a chart replaces that chart's overlay. A new level clears overlays made before it, using an input/frame epoch so that asks opened in `start()` survive `Hooks.play`.
7. **No duplicate titles.** The strip shows each figure's caption on desktop and in the expanded phone strip, so the canvas title would appear twice. `LiveGraph` skips its own title, and the space for it, when the figure's visible `figcaption` already shows the same text. A collapsed phone strip hides captions, so the canvas title is drawn there.
8. **Charts beyond B0.** `deme`, `price`, `tree` and `genome` are built now as simple versions. `genome` had no spec, so I defined one: `{pts: [[pos, y]…], chroms: [{lo, hi, label}], thr: {v, label}, truth: [pos…], prev: [[pos, y]…]}`, binned to one mark per 2 px column (≤ 2,000 marks), with the threshold dashed, hits ringed with a triangle, and truth ticks under the axis. `tree` reads `{nodes, rooted, root, spin: [ids], sel}`.
9. **`Evidence`.** The Brier score is the multi-category form, Σ(p − o)² over hypotheses (0 best, 2 worst). The chosen hypothesis gets `conf` and the rest share `1 − conf`. Purchases are view-level. A chapter whose `sample(n)` draws from `R()` should route purchases through `Sim.act` (`onBuy` is the hook), or replays will diverge. Engine words (days left, power, Conclude…) can be overridden with `o.words`.
10. **`Chips.dist`** divides by the stake, so unplaced chips count as nothing and the best play is to place them all. `Chips.score` and `Chips.expected` are exactly as written.
11. **`Invasion`** also accepts `payoff` (D5's name), `M` (a mutation matrix, passed to `WF.step`) and `from` (restore from `save()`).
12. **`GenePoolLens` key L** is handled through `Hooks.key` for the active lens of the current chapter (`key: null` opts out). A chapter with its own L binding and no lens is unaffected.

## Findings for other packages

- **Architect / `docs/design/v2_chapter_skeleton.js`.** The skeleton asks `Predict.ask({kind: 'pq', start: q})` for the **a** share on a `pq` chart whose bar shows **p** (A on the left). The ghost therefore sits at q measured from the left, not on the A|a split. See the screenshot `skel/skeleton_L0_1280x720_17500ms.png`: the ghost is at 55% from the left while the split is at 45%. Fix either way: pass `axis: {lo: 0, hi: 1, rev: true}`, or ask for p (`start: 1 − q`, expectation 1 − q). Units copying the skeleton will inherit this.
- **E2.** `#strip figcaption` is visible on desktop and in the expanded phone strip, although your log says a typed spec's caption is hidden. LiveGraph now adapts (deviation 7). If you hide captions for typed specs, nothing breaks. `tests/selftest_e2.py` fails 3 checks on the current shared tree ("menu: SVG stars, legacy numerals" ×2 and the `url` run's `…reading 'theme'`). It fails them identically on a build **without** my file (`V2_ONLY='0*,zz_*'`), so they come from the PLAN rewrite in `00_entry.js` or the URL run, not from E5.
- **E6 (Hunt extensions, rigs).** For `lens`, call `GenePoolLens.create({items: () => Hunt items as {x, y, r, a: [a1, a2]}, jar, blocksTaps})` and use `lens.blocks()` to ignore taps. A long press calls `Pin.press(ind, x, y)` and `Pin.release()`; `ind.id` is required and `ind.ph` is copied. Draw `Pin.draw(c, locate, kids)` in your draw; `locate` may return `[x, y, r]`. For `predict: {kind, tol, from}`, call `Predict.ask` with the sim's ask. The commit arrives as `{k: 'predict', id, v}` through `Sim.act`.
- **E4.** `End.show`'s `chart` spec goes through `Hooks.chart(cv, spec, 'paper')`. That is checked: the fixture's level and end cards draw it, and `selftest_e4.js` passes 158/158 on a build that includes this file.
- **E7.** The monkey plays `e5fix` to the end card at 390×780, 1024×768 and 1280×720 on seeds 1–3: drags on the strip, P, arrows, Enter, the dock. The skeleton in your private build also ends, 4/4. `LiveGraph.errors` could join the warnings the monkey fails on.
- **Unit authors.**
  - Open one ask at a time from the sim (`s.ask`), and call `Predict.ask` when its id changes.
  - In untimed mode, pass `soft: null`; Predict also forces it.
  - Call `Predict.reveal(id, expect, tol)` once the sim has scored. `onCommit` is a good place.
  - Score stars from `s.pred` and the sim's expectation, never from `Predict` (the lint forbids view objects inside `sim`).
  - `Predict.score([{err, tol}…])` is the Predict-star helper for view code and tests.
