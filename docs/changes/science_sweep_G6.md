## Considered but NOT changed

- **RECALL.cross, "One map unit is defined as 1% recombination".** Correct for short distances, which is the historical Sturtevant definition. The question says "roughly" and the chapter maths explains Haldane's correction. Kept.
- **TRANSFER.castaways (five founders cannot be exactly 50% blue).** The option reads "about 50%" and the explanation gives correct sampling logic. P(1 or 4 of 5) ≈ 31%, so "could easily be 20% or 80%" is fair. Kept.
- **RECALL.coal, "about N generations".** Correct for N haploid gene copies: P = 1/N per generation, so the mean is N. It matches the chapter (N = 20 copies). Kept.
- **RECALL.hawks (V/C), RECALL.brood (twice as many daughters), RECALL.alarm (0.15 > 0.1), RECALL.lichen (h² = 0.4), RECALL.census (Wahlund), RECALL.casino, RECALL.commons, RECALL.fruits, RECALL.ring, RECALL.jaws, RECALL.fancier, RECALL.sieve, RECALL.plastic, RECALL.mendel (½¹⁰).** All verified, both arithmetically and against chapter code. Kept.
- **Drift Casino cards CQ1/2/5/8.** Checked against `TABLES` in ch1.js. Jar 2 is 10 beads at 0.8; jar 3 is 200 at 0.5; jar 6 is 40 at 0.3 with s = 0.15; jar 9 is 200 with a bottleneck to 2 beads. Fixation probability is 0.8 in neutral jar 2. In jar 6, Kimura gives about 97% eventually, and R7 found 63% within the 30-generation bet. Both are "well above 30%". With the bottleneck, P(fixed at once) = 0.5. All correct. Kept.

- **RECALL.livefast, Abrams (1993) caveat.** Abrams's density-dependence caveat concerns the evolution of senescence. The early-reproduction prediction under high adult mortality is standard (Williams 1966; Reznick guppies), and the chapter cites it. Only "wasted" was softened.

## Needs a gameplay redesign to fix properly

1. **Predictions and recall sit outside play (R3, R8).** TRANSFER hijacks `App.open`, and RECALL fires on every third opening, chosen at random. That gives two or three text cards before the first action, with no tracking, no spacing and no misconception-coded distractors. A rebuild should put prediction inside the simulation (drag a line, tap a triangle) and recall in a spaced, concept-tagged item bank that teachers can see.
2. **MODEL caveats are still missing for about 22 chapters.** These are mendel, scribe, cross, castaways, jaws, hawks, brood, gametes, lek, spindle, meadow, livefast, forager, plastic, switch, copy, sweep, clock, river, ring, tree and web. A consistent per-chapter "model card" (assumptions, invented parameters, what the model cannot show) should be written alongside each chapter's redesign rather than bolted on here.
3. **The Lichen prediction measures tone while asking about camouflage.** A redesign should show the 2D trait space (tone × hue) and compute S and R on the same index the player is asked about.
4. **The Black Country ties the Clean Air Act to player progress.** The MODEL text admits this, but a few generations cannot show the real lag in the decline of carbonaria.
5. **The Sieve secret challenge can only fail with random mating.** Let the player add assortative mating or a large-effect locus, so the link to Two Fruits is played rather than read.
6. **The Lock and Key level 1 framing ("choose which host to target").** The learning layer now speaks of parasite genotypes being favoured, but the level itself still has the parasite choose. It should show parasite genotype frequencies evolving instead.
7. **After the Fire rewards the intermediate disturbance hypothesis as a rule.** Disclosed in MODEL, but the scoring itself needs redesigning (R3).
8. **Clinic: no horizontal gene transfer.** Disclosed in MODEL and RECALL. A plasmid level with a non-TB pathogen is needed (R7_R3 CL4).
