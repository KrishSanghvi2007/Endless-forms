## Considered but NOT changed

- **"Like with like" case is inconsistent** (R4 error 4, second half). Rejected, as R7 A9 also concludes. At p = 0.5, `phenAssort` keeps AA = aa. The trace matches about 75% colour assortment, which is what "almost only" says.
- **"Codominant data would let the player count genotypes"** (R4 L17). Rejected (R7 A39). All three L17 datasets are already codominant counts. The real gap is a dominant-trait dataset and a low-n sample (see the redesign list).
- **L3: "the puzzle Hardy was asked to solve in 1908."** Kept. Yule's 3:1 remark (1908) and Hardy's reply (*Science* 28:49, 1908) make it correct (R7 B7). R4's "Yule 1902" date is R4's slip, not the game's.
- **L13 diversity curve mixes expected and realised H** (`min(He, 2p(1−p))`, R7 C6). Kept. The label "share of heterozygotes expected" is a fair name for 2pq from the current p. Showing realised 2pq alone at one biallelic locus can rise by chance and would confuse the lesson. The honest fix is the multi-allele redesign.
- **L8's 3% mutation rate and m = 0.15.** Labelled, not changed. A realistic rate (10⁻⁵) would be invisible in 12 generations. m = 0.15 is high but plausible.
- **Sickle-cell counts (25,374 / 5,482 / 67).** I could not trace the source: web searches found no primary paper, and the likely textbook page was blocked by the proxy. R4's attribution to Allison 1954 is wrong (R7 B29), so I did not add it. The note stays as "a classic survey from the 1950s". The χ² and O/E values are recomputed and correct.
- **Scarlet tiger counts (15,784 / 1,221 / 28).** Could not be checked against Ford & Sheppard (1969) offline. χ² recomputed (0.75). I kept "the three wing forms are the three genotypes". That is the standard reading; Goulson & Owen (1997) argued that temperature affects expression, and Cook & Jones (1996) disputed it.
- **CF "about 1 in 2,500" for Northern European ancestry.** Kept. The cited range is about 1 in 2,000 to 1 in 3,500, and the UK figure is about 1 in 2,500. The 1-in-25 carrier figure follows.
- **L10's "realistic rate of about 1 in 100,000".** Kept. That is a fair per-gene rate (R7 B12). Per-nucleotide rates are about 10⁻⁸, but the level is about a gene.
- **L9: "after 20 generations q = 0.045, not zero."** Kept. It is the correct deterministic recursion q/(1 + q) for a large population, and it is framed that way.
- **L4: "Random mating is random drawing."** Kept. Strictly this is random union of gametes. It is equivalent when the sexes have the same allele frequencies, and L7 now states that condition.
- **L15: "selection can move it along the curve while it stays in proportion".** Kept. "Can" is accurate (true when newborns are censused). L8 now shows the adult heterozygote excess.
- **Case 7: "Evolution is a change in allele frequencies."** Kept. It is the standard population-genetics definition and fits a Hardy–Weinberg course.
- **F (inbreeding coefficient) never named** (R4, R7 A33). This is an omission, not an error, and it needs a level (see below). Adding it to the already full L14 card would overload it.
- **L17's third, fictional dataset in "Real populations".** Kept. It is labelled "A game population".
- **Castaways case trace (0.5 → 0.8 from 6 founders, not a multiple of 1/12).** Kept. The first plotted value is the mainland, and island sizes after founding are not stated.
- **Ferry case.** Its implied m (about 0.15–0.2) is consistent with about 50 migrants a year into a few hundred. Kept.
- **Title-card line "Each beetle wears its genes on its wings"** (`shell.html`). Not my file, and harmless.
- **`engine.js`.** It holds no player-facing science text beyond axis labels, and its genetics helpers (`hwExp`, `pOf`, `hwCounts`, `wfNext`, `chi2`) are correct. No changes.

## Needs a gameplay redesign to fix properly

1. **L14 non-random mating.** The player should see disassortative mating move p: a run from p = 0.8, or colour-based disassortment, which drives the population to 1 Aa : 1 aa, as in *Primula* heterostyly. Name F (H = 2pq(1 − F)). Show that colour assortment leaves an unlinked locus on the curve while inbreeding lowers both. L8 (genotype assortment, mixed 60:40 with random mating), L14 (colour assortment) and L15 (70% colour assortment) use three different "non-random mating" models.
2. **L13 bottleneck.** A single biallelic locus at p = 0.5 cannot show the main effect, the loss of rare alleles. Use a multi-allele locus. The castaway choice still gives a free point (`const ok = true; this.score++`).
3. **L12 drift challenge.** It does not reward small N: any N passes, and N of 20–30 is a coin toss. Nₑ is now mentioned but never played (sex ratio, fluctuating size, Buri's 107 bottles giving Nₑ ≈ 11.5 from 16).
4. **L8 mysteries and L15.** Each label is used once, so the last answer is free. Every force has one fixed, exaggerated strength and direction. Mutation is excluded. The ✓/✗ double negatives ("✗ No selection") confuse.
5. **L9.** Peek, memorise, then hunt is still possible: harmless to the score (R7 A7) but conceptually leaky. Static beetles, no real background, no contrast between selection on a dominant and on a recessive, no Δq = −spq²/(1 − sq²).
6. **L10.** Inert. Mutation–selection balance (q̂ ≈ √(μ/s) against μ/s) is the missing payoff, and the end card now hints at it only in L9.
7. **L11.** Nothing opposes migration (no migration–selection balance, no swamping).
8. **L17.** Power is never felt (both real samples have N > 17,000). There is no dominant-trait dataset where HW must be assumed, and the sickle-cell source is unverified.
9. **Case Files.** Each prompt states its own answer ("Mark–recapture…", "a ferry service began").
10. **Deterministic and stochastic mixed without labels.** `hwCounts` is used in some levels, sampling in others. Show expected as a ghost and realised as a solid.
11. **Missing concepts.** Heterozygote advantage at equilibrium (p constant, adults above the curve); X-linkage (spiral approach); multiple alleles (ABO); LD decay; F_ST; Haldane's sieve and 2s fixation.
12. **L7's conditions** are now small print. Better: a boss round with unequal sex frequencies or an X-linked gene that does not reach HW in one step.
13. **Shipped file.** `games/equilibrium-hw/Equilibrium_HW_v2.html` (the shipped single-file build, outside this group) still has the old text until someone copies the new build there.
